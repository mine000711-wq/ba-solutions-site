AEGIS-V Vehicle Jammer

World's first liquid-cooled vehicle RCIED jammer — no sound, no heat, no operation-time limit.

OVERVIEW

AEGIS-V is a heavy-duty vehicle-mounted RCIED jammer installed in the trunk of a vehicle, the rear of a truck, or inside an armored vehicle. Using proprietary digital barrage jamming, it disrupts a wide band from 20MHz to 6GHz to disable a broad range of remote-controlled IEDs — protecting a 50m to 1km radius around the vehicle depending on the radio environment.

OUR PROMISE

All AEGIS-V jammers run on the vehicle's DC alternator as main power with a backup battery as auxiliary — so there is no operation-time limit. As long as the vehicle runs, the jammer keeps running, and there is no need to replace or recharge the battery every day.

WORLD-FIRST LIQUID COOLING

60°C outside. 20–30°C inside.

AEGIS-V is the world's first liquid-cooled vehicle jammer. A fully enclosed liquid loop holds the internal amplifier temperature at 20–30°C even during a 60°C chamber test — with no cooling fan, no exposed heat sink, and no heat vented into the vehicle. The result is a sealed, water- and dust-proof machine that stays quiet and cool under sustained, heavy-duty operation.
