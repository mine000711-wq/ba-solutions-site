ROK Army Drone Combat Challenge 2026

2026 / 07 / 29

BA Solutions advances to the finals of the Drone Combat and Defense Competition hosted by the South Korean Ministry of National Defense (MND).

We are proud to announce that our team has successfully passed the preliminary round and advanced to the finals in the Drone Combat and Defense Competition hosted by the South Korean Ministry of National Defense (MND).

Competing in the defense category, we introduced a highly proactive and innovative counter-strategy: neutralizing the threat by preventing the opposing forces from launching their drones in the first place. This advanced tactical approach drew significant interest and acclaim from both judges and defense experts.

Looking ahead to the main competition, we are fully committed to enhancing our defensive strategies and sharpening our core C-UAS technologies. Leveraging our proven track record with elite law enforcement, we are ready to compete for the championship at the MND-hosted finals. Thank you for your continued support.
