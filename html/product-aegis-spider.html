AEGIS Spider

Non-destructive drone capture via precision net deployment.

OVERVIEW

AEGIS Spider is a soft-kill interceptor that captures hostile drones intact using precision net deployment. Powered by AEGIS AI and the DDE edge chip, it classifies, tracks, and engages autonomously — neutralizing threats without kinetic destruction, ideal where debris, forensics, or populated airspace rule out hard-kill.
